03 — Evidence Method

PRESERVE FIRST.
EXAMINE SECOND.
CONCLUDE LAST.

The archive distinguishes documentary records from claims, allegations, inferences, interpretations, speculation and spiritual belief.

CLAIM What is asserted?
SOURCE Where did it come from?
DOCUMENT What record exists?
RESPONSE What response is recorded?
STATUS What can presently be established?
A

Documented Fact

Supported directly by an identifiable primary record or official source.

B

Corroborated Claim

A claim supported by multiple relevant sources.

C

Documented Allegation

An allegation recorded in documentary material but not established as fact.

D

Inference / Interpretation

An interpretation drawn from available records.

E

Speculation

A possibility or hypothesis requiring further evidence.

—

Unresolved

The available material does not presently establish a conclusion.

ARCHIVE NOTICE

This archive is not a court and does not issue legal verdicts. It does not determine criminal guilt, replace legal advice, or independently establish every claim presented in the underlying documentary corpus. Allegations remain allegations unless independently established. Faith and spiritual interpretation are presented separately from documentary findings.
