03 — Evidence Method

PRESERVE FIRST.
EXAMINE SECOND.
CONCLUDE LAST.

The archive distinguishes documentary records from claims, allegations, inferences, interpretations, speculation and spiritual belief.

CLAIM → SOURCE → DOCUMENT → RESPONSE → OUTCOME → STATUS
A

Documented Fact

Supported directly by an identifiable primary record or official source.

B

Corroborated Claim

A claim supported by multiple relevant sources.

C

Documented Allegation

An allegation recorded in documentary material but not established as fact.

D

Inference / Interpretation

An interpretation drawn from available records.

E

Speculation

A possibility or hypothesis requiring further evidence.

IMPORTANT:
This archive is not a court and does not issue legal verdicts. An allegation remains an allegation unless independently established. Faith and spiritual interpretation are presented separately from documentary findings.

08 — Justice Mandate

TRUTH DESERVES
PRESERVATION.

The project calls for careful examination of records, independent scrutiny where appropriate, accountability where evidence supports it, and dignity for people whose lives become subjects of institutional records.

A person should not be reduced to the worst thing written about them.

The archive is offered as a resource for journalists, lawyers, researchers, historians, advocates, investigators, academics, public officials and members of the public.
