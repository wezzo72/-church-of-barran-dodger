Reading the record carefully

Evidence, Testimony and Belief

An important principle of this project is that different kinds of statements should be identified according to their source and status.

A

Documented Fact

A proposition directly established by an identifiable source, record or document.

B

Corroborated Claim

A claim supported by additional independent or otherwise relevant material, without necessarily constituting an official determination.

C

Documented Allegation

An allegation recorded in a document, correspondence, statement or other source. Recording an allegation does not establish it as fact.

D

Inference

A conclusion or interpretation drawn from available material, rather than something directly stated by the underlying source.

E

Speculation

A possibility that remains insufficiently established and should not be presented as an established fact.

Faith

Spiritual Belief

Religious belief, biblical interpretation, prophecy or spiritual testimony is identified as such rather than presented as documentary proof.

Impartial reading notice: A government document is not the same thing as an interpretation of that document. Personal testimony is not automatically an official finding. An allegation is not a determination of fact. A spiritual belief is not documentary proof. A reconstructed archive is not independent corroboration of the original record.
