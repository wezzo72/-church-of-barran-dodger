4. The Documentary Chain

The appropriate evidentiary path is not: AI calculation → fact.

It is a longer chain requiring examination at each stage:

PRIMARY RECORD → EXPENDITURE → DATE → AGENCY → CONNECTION → CALCULATION → INDEPENDENT REVIEW

Any weak or unsupported link in that chain affects the reliability of the resulting estimate.

5. AI Is an Analytical Tool — Not an Adjudicator

Machine learning and artificial intelligence can assist with searching, classification, extraction, pattern recognition and aggregation across a large documentary corpus.

They can also introduce errors through incomplete datasets, duplicated records, incorrect classification, mistaken attribution, assumptions embedded in prompts or models, and other methodological limitations.

AI-generated analysis may identify a question worthy of investigation. It does not, by itself, convert an allegation into a fact or establish legal responsibility.
