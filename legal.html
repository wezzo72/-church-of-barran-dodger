01 · Purpose

Follow the administrative record.

This section brings together legal and administrative material that may help readers reconstruct decisions, correspondence, procedures, reviews, applications, complaints, responses and other institutional interactions contained within the archive.

The purpose is documentary reconstruction. It is not to substitute an archival finding for a court judgment or legal determination.

IMPORTANT: A document describing a legal dispute, allegation, complaint or administrative process does not itself establish that the underlying allegation was proven. Where an authoritative determination exists, it should be identified as such. Where no determination has been located, that absence should remain visible.

04 · Legal Status

A record is not automatically a ruling.

The archive distinguishes between a person's allegation, an institution's record of that allegation, an administrative decision, a court finding and an interpretation made later by an author or reader.

DOCUMENTED

A record exists

The underlying document can be identified and located.

DETERMINED

An authority made a finding

Where supported by the source, an authoritative determination can be identified separately from the allegations that preceded it.

ALLEGED

A proposition was asserted

The record documents what a person or institution alleged, without treating the allegation as established fact.

DISPUTED

The proposition is contested

Conflicting accounts or evidence should remain visible rather than being silently resolved.

UNRESOLVED

No responsible conclusion

The presently available material does not establish an appropriate conclusion.

NO FINDING LOCATED

No authoritative finding found

No relevant authoritative determination has been located in the material examined so far.

06 · Independent Examination

The archive does not decide the case.

The Witness Remains is a public documentary archive and finding aid. It does not determine criminal guilt, civil liability, administrative responsibility or legal entitlement.

The strongest purpose of preservation is to make the underlying material available for independent examination by people with the appropriate legal, professional, investigative or research expertise.

NOT A COURT · NOT A VERDICT

Nothing on this page should be understood as a substitute for legal advice, professional investigation, judicial determination or independent review.
